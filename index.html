Why it matters

Memorising answers won’t protect your child’s future

AI can already recall any fact, formula or answer in a second. A child who only learns by rote is learning to do what machines already do better.

A child who understands concepts can think, question and solve problems they have never seen before. That is what we teach at KIMS.

How your child learns

Concept-based learning, not mindless memorisation

In our Montessori classrooms, children learn the way they learn best: by doing. Ideas are explored with real materials, at your child’s own pace, until they truly make sense.

  • Maths they can see and touchChildren count, build and compare real objects, so numbers mean something long before they become sums on paper.
  • Reading with understandingChildren learn the sounds letters make and build their own words, so they read with understanding, not by repetition.
  • Hands busy, minds switched onScience, nature, cooking, music and movement are all hands-on, so learning sticks.
  • Happy, confident, independentWe believe a happy child is the surest sign that a child’s needs are being met. Our children grow in confidence and independence every day.
