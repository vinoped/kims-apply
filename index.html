Why KIMS

Happiness first, learning that lasts

  • Rooted in the principles of Dr. Maria Montessori and the vision of Rabindranath Tagore, following how children naturally develop.
  • Mixed-age classrooms for children aged about 3 to 5, with individual work and learning from peers.
  • Hands-on learning with concrete materials for maths, movable alphabets for language, and sensorial objects for shapes and space.
  • Science & Nature
  • Music & Movement
  • Sensorial
  • Cooking & Nutrition
  • Cultural Awareness
  • History & Geography
  • Language Arts
  • Mathematics
Class 6 to Class 12: KIMS Higher Secondary School is being developed and will open soon. Only students from KIMS Primary School will be admitted; no new admissions are being taken for Classes 6 to 12.
