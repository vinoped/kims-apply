Concept-based learning, not mindless memorisation

Children at KIMS learn by doing. They work with real materials, at their own pace, until an idea truly makes sense to them.

  • Maths they can holdChildren see, touch and count real quantities, so numbers mean something instead of being symbols to memorise.
  • Words they build themselvesWith movable alphabet letters, children put sounds together to make words and discover how language works.
  • Learning through the sensesSensorial materials help children understand shape, size and space by exploring them with their hands.
  • Montessori + TagoreRooted in the principles of Dr. Maria Montessori and the vision of Rabindranath Tagore, following how children naturally grow.

Along the way, children grow in independence, confidence and creative thinking.
