Privacy policy

Admissions enquiries · Last updated 6 October 2026

This explains how Kudos Indian Montessori Shikshalaya (KIMS), Selaiyur, Chennai, an initiative of The VEDIC Educational and Charitable Trust, uses the details you share when you enquire about admission.

What we collect

  • Your details: your name, your mobile number and the class you are enquiring about.
  • On apply.kimsindia.com: we save these details as you type them, so we can still contact you if you stop before pressing submit. We also note whether you tapped our call button, whether you used a phone or a computer, and which website or advert brought you to the page.
  • On Facebook and Instagram forms: the answers you submit in the form.

Why we collect it

Only to contact you about admission to KIMS: to answer your questions and arrange a school visit or interview. We do not sell your details or use them for anything unrelated to admissions.

Where it is stored

  • Enquiries from apply.kimsindia.com are stored in our website system, provided by Wix.
  • Enquiries from Facebook and Instagram forms are stored by Meta, which provides those forms, and are available to our admissions team.
  • The map on our page is provided by Google Maps.

These providers handle the data on our behalf under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as we need them for admissions, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the details we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Contact us using the details below and we will act on your request.
